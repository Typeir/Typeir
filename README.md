## About

Hi!

I am David, I've been a software engineer for about a decade, and have been through jQuery, the rise of React, the fall of Angular, the microservice craze, and much more.

I am a two-time national champion in competitive programming for my degree, and have a few more achievements under my belt; this is not a brag, it is just a receipt.

In recent years I've been focusing more on LLMs and AI, and assistive technologies, and all of my projects use them on a daily basis. I have enough experience in **manual coding** to warrant _parting ways with it_ for the most part; I've fixed critical infrastructure in some of the world's largest banks and businesses, and have designed testing strategies for products so hostile you wouldn't want to know about them.

**Despite** all of this, I appreciate _human labour and artistry_ a lot; I won't let generative tools interject themselves into art, illustration, modelling or design of any kind. I am of the opinion that the **Hard Problem of Consciousness** is **unsolvable**; therefore all that's left to me is the art I make and the making of it. I can't let agents replace that. The art in code is sparse, it is abundant _elsewhere_.

(Sorry for the Aphorism, I promise I'm a real person)
